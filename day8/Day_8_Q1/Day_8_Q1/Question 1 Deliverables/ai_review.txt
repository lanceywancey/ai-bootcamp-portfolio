Why AI-generated code may fail: 
AI can misunderstand the requirements, miss edge cases, or produce code that looks correct but does not actually work.

Why automated verification is important: 
Tests and Jenkins confirm whether the code works and help detect bugs after changes.

Strengths and weaknesses of AI tools: 
AI is useful for generating ideas, test cases, and debugging suggestions, but its answers can still be incorrect and must be reviewed.

Why subsystem decomposition is useful: 
Splitting the system into smaller parts makes testing easier and helps identify exactly where the problem is.
